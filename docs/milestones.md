## Completion rule

A day is complete only when the learner has studied the lesson, performed the relevant implementation, completed the exercises, checked the results against independent expectations, and recorded actual observations in that day's engineering notebook.

A companion script, reference figure, or prebuilt model being present in the repository is not evidence that the learner has completed that lesson.
